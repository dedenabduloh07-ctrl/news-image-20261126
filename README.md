A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Thursday 26 November 2026, built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126) bounty **news-image-20261126**.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is a dramatic front page in deep navy and fire orange:

- **Lead story — Cairo fuel tanker explosion.** A fuel tanker overturned and exploded on the Ring Road in Giza, killing 31 people and charring 27 vehicles. The big cinematic panel shows the tanker fireball at night: orange glow, black smoke column, charred vehicles on the highway.
- **Hormuz** — a supertanker carrying over 2 million barrels was hit by a mine near Khasab at the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Kyiv** — Russian missiles struck a shopping centre in the Desnianskyi district, wounding seven.
- **Thanksgiving storm** — a powerful nor'easter knocked out power to nearly 900,000 US homes and businesses and delayed 2,000+ flights.
- **US tariffs** — proposed new tariffs on 14 economies, including China capped at 20%.
- **World in brief** — Gaza strikes kill 9; a Houthi projectile hits Jizan Port; the Bogor gold mine flood leaves 13 dead and 13 missing; UK net migration falls to 77,000; Kushner calls Putin a "grandmaster of politics".

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) copies the committed `entry.png` to `/output`. Nothing else is generated at run time — the image is the entry.

```
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.png
```
